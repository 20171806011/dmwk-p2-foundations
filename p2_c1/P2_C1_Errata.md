## Preimplementation clarifications

- The compiler reserves a proved rank-three containing space even for programs whose complete generated-call envelope has smaller rank or no calls. The latter is not a sampled estimate and is not necessarily the reachable-state span from a proper initial subset.
- The physical program is its graph, wires and shared bank together. A fixed 2-by-3 gate bank is not a constant-size encoding of arbitrary programs; site metadata, work and state/wire storage grow with the source.
- The supported structural mask is an exact Frobenius projection onto the editable block. General geometry is not thereby BIT-certified, and arbitrary rotations do not commute with coordinate ReLU.
- Initial A anchor requests are initial assignments, not protected tasks that future learning must preserve. Exact anchor representability and approximate optimizer proposals have different contracts.
- The finite endpoint count N_F^(m*c) applies to the chosen structural universe and one-hot A observation domain. It is not a count of the unrestricted all-matrix BIT class or a promise of useful learning.
- The new compiled source/layout needs its own authorization gate. Pure older arithmetic/optimizer helpers do not extend B1's fixed model registry to arbitrary graphs.
- Successful updates preserve D's designated observations; rejection preserves the entire owned snapshot. Numerical or geometric read-only records cannot authorize a live update.

## Verifier correction and implementation limit

During review of the first compiler test run, the ReLU rotation example was recorded with hardcoded distinct outputs, and a guard-threshold example lacked a direct calculation. The final verifier computes the rotation, both coordinatewise ReLU compositions and the decoded threshold crossing. The corrected compiler run reports 319,910 assertions. The original proof, compiler and previous research files required no change. The primary and foreign evidence use the corrected verifier.

The serial owner parses candidate text up to 16 MB. Unbounded availability does not follow from D trace preservation: an oversized candidate can fail at commit after an earlier prepare. Until a successful publication, the full previous owner remains live. The allowed number of steps in the learning experiment fits this reference budget.
