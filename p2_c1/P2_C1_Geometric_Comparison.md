# P2-C1 geometric comparison on a compiled program

## Derivation before implementation

Consider a compiled program whose next state and observation each equal `and(1,1)`. The compiler emits two calls to the same physical bank. Both legal initial states and both Boolean external inputs are admitted. Each call prepares exactly the vector h=(1,1,1,0), including the homogeneous one and the reserved A coordinate. Thus the reachable and all-state call spans both equal span(h) of rank one. This equality follows from the source and its closed lowering rule. Changing a fixed gate coefficient in this experiment requires a separate evaluation of the affected compiled reduction.

The exact projection onto directions orthogonal to h is I−hhᵀ/3. The coupled edit (1,−1,0,0) to the AND row already lies in this plane. It leaves the A block, biases, NOT row and input preparations unchanged. The new AND row begins (2,0,−1). Its two nonzero terms on h give 2−1=1, and every product and partial sum is exactly representable in both formats. Although an internal partial sum changes, both preactivations after reduction, both ReLU outputs, the selected results and the full next state and output words agree. Every initial state/input pair and each of the two calls satisfies this argument, hence all finite ticks agree under the T1–T3 state/schedule premises. The exact projection formula specifies a subspace over the rationals. Execution never evaluates the projector coefficients as floating-point numbers.

On the **same source, span, graph and initial endpoints**, set the AND row to (F,1,−F) where F=2^p and p is 8 for BF16 or 24 for FP32. Its exact dot product on h is also one, and its nonzero update has rank one. However, `(F+1)` is a tie that rounds to F, and the final subtraction yields zero. The two edits thus share the exact annihilation condition and differ on the fixed rounded graph. The structural owner rejects each edit because it changes immutable cells. Neither geometric record authorizes a write.

## Diagnostic scope

`verify_geometry.py` reconstructs the artifact against the supplied full source and budgets. The diagnostic starts from the original admissible endpoint. It accepts comparisons only for canonical finite, shape-matched alternative endpoints that leave every cell outside the 2-by-3 gate block unchanged. It enumerates every Boolean state and input and all compiler-generated D calls. It computes each bank's own wire values, then compares site inputs, completed preactivations, post-ReLU outputs, selected results, next-state words and source output words. Its BIT predicate covers those data and excludes internal multiply/add partial sums, which the program does not expose. Failure to finish a comparison, an invalid endpoint or a non-Boolean gate result causes diagnostic refusal. A failing comparison can instead return a concrete difference for two total Boolean machines.

Exact matrix subtraction uses rational decoding of the two stored endpoints. Annihilation is checked on every generator of the full original call envelope. The resulting ALG flag applies to that envelope. The structural compiler separately uses the conservative rank-three ambient space for live authorization. Requiring unchanged A and cross-block cells preserves the original A graph. Full finite state/input quantification, same initial state and input schedule and equality of the joint one-step state/output table suffice to lift a passing comparison to every finite D trajectory of this closed machine. All receipts are read-only diagnostics, carry explicitly scoped observation and provenance, and make no update, optimizer or combined-kernel claim.
