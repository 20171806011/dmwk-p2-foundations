# P2-A4 — Rank budgets, response feasibility and finite-format limits

All statements use [Definitions](P2_A4_Definitions.md). They are mathematical proofs, not conclusions extrapolated from finite tests. Let n,d>=1, K=Q or R, V subset K^d have rank r, and c=d-r. Whenever stored words are discussed, decoding, endpoint family and execution contracts are fixed explicitly.

## T1 — Exact freedom, rank budget and attainment

**Statement.** The exact update space U(V) has dimension nc. Every Delta in U(V) has rank at most min(n,c). Let P=P_perp be the exact complementary projector and k>=0 an integer. If Delta=GP with rank(G)<=k, then

\[
\operatorname{rank}\Delta\le\min(k,n,c).
\]

Conversely, for every integer q with 0<=q<=min(k,n,c), an abstract update of this form has rank q. Thus the upper bound is attained when unconstrained K-valued G may be chosen freely. For square matrices n=d, these become dimension d(d-r), rank at most d-r, and rank at most min(k,d-r).

**Proof.** Choose a basis v_1,...,v_r of V and extend it to a basis by c vectors z_1,...,z_c. An annihilating map must send every v_i to zero and may send each z_j to an arbitrary vector of K^n. These n c scalar choices are independent, proving the dimension. Its image is generated by the c chosen images, and lies in K^n, so its rank is at most min(n,c).

The rank of a product is at most the rank of either factor, hence rank(GP)<=min(rank(G),rank(P))<=min(k,n,c). To attain q, choose q independent output vectors y_1,...,y_q; define Delta z_i=y_i for i<=q and Delta z_i=0 otherwise, while Delta V=0. This has rank q. Since x=(I-P)x+Px with (I-P)x in V, Delta x=Delta Px for every x. Thus Delta=Delta P. Taking G=Delta gives the required factorization and rank(G)=q<=k. For q=0 use the zero map. ∎

At r=d, c=0 and U(V)={0}. At r=0 all n-by-d maps are allowed abstractly. Positive rank is attainable exactly when n>0, c>0 and k>=1 under the cap. Additional masks, fixed factor spaces, coefficient restrictions, finite-format endpoints or executable safety can make attainment fail; none is covered by the unconstrained construction.

**Fixed-generator orientation.** For a specified G of rank g,

\[
\max(0,g-r)\le\operatorname{rank}(GP)\le\min(g,c).
\]

The upper bound was proved above. For the lower bound, the domain decomposes as V plus V-perp, so im(G) is the sum of G(V) and G(V-perp). Consequently g<=rank(G|_V)+rank(G|_(V-perp))<=r+rank(GP). This proves the inequality. In particular a nonzero G whose row space lies in V annihilates V-perp and gives GP=0. A rank budget is potential freedom, not a guarantee that a given proposal realizes it.

**Low-rank family is not generally linear.** Identify U(V) with n-by-c matrices in the chosen basis. If 0<k<min(n,c), one rank-k diagonal block and one rank-one diagonal entry in a disjoint position each have rank<=k, but their sum has rank k+1. Therefore U_<=k(V) is not a vector space in that regime. The degenerate k=0 family is {0}; when k>=min(n,c) the constraint is inactive and the full linear space is recovered. No linear dimension nc is attributed to the constrained non-linear set.

## T2 — Protection growth, shared calls and provenance

**Statement.** If V subset U subset K^d with ranks r<=r', then

\[
\mathcal U(U)\subseteq\mathcal U(V),\qquad
\dim\mathcal U(V)-\dim\mathcal U(U)=n(r'-r).
\]

The maximum attainable unconstrained rank decreases from min(n,d-r) to min(n,d-r'); under the generator cap it becomes min(k,n,d-r'). The **unrestricted exact annihilator space** is completely exhausted iff r'=d, assuming n>=1. A cap k=0 or further constraints can exhaust a constrained family earlier. For finitely many shared sites, V=sum_j V_j and U(V)=intersection_j U(V_j).

**Proof.** A map vanishing on U vanishes on its subset V, proving inclusion. Apply T1's dimension/rank formulas. U(V)={0} iff n(d-r)=0, which with n>=1 is equivalent to r=d. A map vanishes on a sum precisely when it vanishes on every summand: linearity proves one direction, and containment of each summand proves the other. ∎

The capacity cost of a new task depends on independent new protected directions, not its name or the number of examples. If a new task's activations already lie in V, the algebraic space is unchanged. In K^2, two sites separately generating e_1 and e_2 each leave one complement direction, but their shared union is full rank and leaves no exact nonzero update.

**Containing and sampled spaces.** If the true global span is V and a proved containing space U has rank r'>r, using U loses n(r'-r) abstract parameters and may remove all freedom. The remaining maps are safe algebraically for the true set, but some safe maps are conservatively rejected. Conversely a legal sample span S subset V of rank s gives n(d-s)>=n(d-r), an optimistic upper bound on abstract freedom. It cannot certify that those directions protect omitted actual inputs. These are inclusion results; a sample matrix that independently proves full coverage may be relabeled only after that proof is supplied.

**Finite-set monotonicity.** At fixed old bank, endpoint family and scalar policy, E_ALG(w,U) is contained in E_ALG(w,V) when V subset U. At fixed old bank, graph, numeric policy, endpoint family and effects, E_BIT(w,Omega') is contained in E_BIT(w,Omega) when Omega subset Omega'. The first follows by annihilation; the second follows because equality/totality on a larger set implies it on its subset. Cardinalities and any response-image sets on a fixed A target list therefore cannot increase. Mere equality or inclusion of numeric spans does not imply this BIT inclusion: the raw legal contexts must actually be included. These statements do not compare changing old banks, numerical policies or target sets during unrestricted training.

## T3 — Target-response capacity and prescribed-response feasibility

Let X in K^(d by m), m>=0, list the specified A target inputs. Let pi:K^d->K^d/V be the quotient map and let Y=pi X in a chosen basis of that c-dimensional quotient. Set

\[
s=\operatorname{rank}Y=\operatorname{rank}[H\ X]-r,
\]

where H generates V exactly. The response map is rho_X(Delta)=Delta X on U(V).

**Statement.** The image of rho_X has dimension n s, and its kernel has dimension n(c-s). A prescribed R in K^(n by m) is attainable iff

\[
\ker Y\subseteq\ker R,
\quad\text{equivalently}\quad
X\alpha\in V\ \Longrightarrow\ R\alpha=0.
\]

When feasible, the minimum possible rank of an implementing Delta is rank(R). Under a generator budget rank(G)<=k with Delta=GP, feasibility is equivalent to the same relation condition together with rank(R)<=k.

**Proof of image and dimension.** Every annihilator Delta factors uniquely through pi as Delta=L pi, for a K-linear map L:K^d/V->K^n: define L([x])=Delta x, which is well-defined because representatives differ by V. Conversely every L produces an annihilator. Thus responses are LY. The image of Y has dimension s; only L restricted to that image affects LY. That restriction may be any map from an s-dimensional space to K^n. Distinct restrictions give distinct LY because Y maps onto its image. There are n s independent scalar choices, proving response-image dimension. By rank-nullity of the linear map rho_X, its kernel dimension is nc-ns=n(c-s). Equivalently silent maps annihilate V+col(X), whose rank is r+s.

**Proof of feasibility and minimum rank.** If R=LY and Y alpha=0, then R alpha=0, proving necessity. Conversely suppose ker Y subset ker R. Define L_0 on im(Y) by L_0(Y alpha)=R alpha. If Y alpha=Y beta, their difference is in ker Y and hence in ker R, so the definition is independent of the chosen coefficient representation. It is linear. Its image is exactly im(R). Choose a complementary subspace to im(Y) in K^d/V and define L=0 on that complement while extending L_0. Then im(L)=im(R), so rank(L)=rank(R). Since pi is onto, Delta=L pi has the same image/rank, annihilates V and satisfies Delta X=R. Every implementing Delta must satisfy rank(R)=rank(Delta X)<=rank(Delta), proving minimality.

If rank(G)<=k, T1 gives rank(Delta)<=k, so rank(R)<=k is necessary. For the constructed Delta of rank(R), set G=Delta; Delta P=Delta and rank(G)=rank(R)<=k, proving sufficiency. All constructions use K-linear basis extensions and can be rational when H,X,R are rational. ∎

**Maximum response rank.** Among feasible responses with rank cap k, the maximum rank is min(k,n,s), attained by sending that many independent quotient-target vectors to independent output vectors and all other quotient directions to zero. The minimum implementing rank theorem shows this response is realized by an update of the same rank.

Equivalently, a response of rank q can be realized on some target response assignment iff 0<=q<=min(k,n,s), with the cap omitted when inactive. Reserving c>=q directions alone is insufficient if the actual targets have quotient rank s<q. For a particular prescribed assignment, the relation condition remains necessary in addition to its rank bound.

**Nonzero response and target silence.** Without an active rank cap, a changed exact target output exists iff s>0, because n>=1. Under the cap, also require k>=1. A nonzero safe exact update that is silent on all targets exists iff c>s, and under a cap requires k>=1. Necessity follows from the kernel dimension and sufficiency from a rank-one map on one quotient direction outside the target quotient span. When s=c, rho_X is injective: every nonzero annihilator changes at least one selected target. When s=0 every target lies in V and every annihilator is silent.

**Arbitrary target labels.** Every R in K^(n by m) is feasible without a cap iff Y has full column rank m. If ker Y is nonzero, choose a nonzero relation alpha and an R with R alpha nonzero, which is impossible. If ker Y={0}, the relation condition is vacuous. To realize every such R under a generator cap requires k>=min(n,m), because arbitrary matrices R can have that maximum rank; this condition is sufficient by the proved construction. In particular independently adjustable target directions require c>=m, but c>=m alone is insufficient if their quotient classes are dependent.

**A small impossibility example.** Let V=span(e_1) in K^3 and X=[e_2,2e_2]. The quotient target rank is one. Every response has second column twice its first; asking for responses (y,0) with y nonzero is impossible, although a nonzero safe response exists. Choosing Delta e_2=y and Delta e_1=Delta e_3=0 realizes (y,2y). A nonzero map supported only on e_3 is safe but silent on these targets. This distinguishes update existence, target reachability and independent target control.

All statements in this theorem concern exact linear response capacity, not useful task learning or storable endpoints. A finite-domain BIT edit may change an A input lying in V, because it need not be an exact annihilator at all.

## T4 — Discrete endpoint counts and their limits

Fix canonical finite alphabet size N=N_F, old stored W and a finite endpoint family E. Let q be the dimension of an affine solution space containing the decoded E_ALG endpoints; for unrestricted exact annihilation q=n(d-r). Optional fixed-coordinate linear constraints may reduce q.

**Statement.**

\[
|\mathcal E_{\rm ALG}|\le\min(|\mathcal E|,N^q).
\]

If the no-op w is admitted, |E_ALG|>=1. At full protected rank with no bias edit, E_ALG={w} whenever w is in E. The corresponding finite effective-update set has the same cardinality under canonical decoding, but is not a nonzero K-vector space in general.

**Proof.** Write the affine solution space as x_0+L in K^(nd), where dim L=q. A basis matrix for L has column rank q, so some q rows form an invertible q-by-q minor. Projection to those q coordinate positions is injective on L, hence on x_0+L. Each projected stored coordinate can take at most N values. There are at most N^q such tuples, proving the count, and E itself gives the other bound. If q=0 there is at most one point. At r=d, T1 forces decoded W'=W; canonical encoding is injective, so there is exactly the admitted no-op. At fixed W, subtraction is injective on decoded endpoints, proving the effective-update count statement. A finite subset with a nonzero vector cannot be a vector space over the infinite fields Q or R, because its scalar multiples would be infinite. ∎

With heterogeneous coordinate alphabets, the same proof bounds the count by the product of their sizes over any q-coordinate injective projection, and one may take the smallest such bound. This does not assume that all projected combinations extend to representable endpoints. Rank caps are non-linear constraints; they can reduce the feasible count but do not automatically justify replacing q by a dimension assigned to an unrelated determinantal set.

**The bound is attainable.** Suppose V is the span of the first r standard basis vectors, every coefficient can use all N canonical finite words, and fixed old W is admitted. Exact annihilation fixes the first r endpoint columns equal to W and leaves all n c remaining words independently selectable. Thus exactly N^(nc) endpoints are algebraically feasible. Execution and A-response conditions are still separate unless a graph argument is added.

**Response counts.** For any finite feasible endpoint subset S, exact response-image cardinality is at most |S|. If every endpoint in S is accepted on m specified raw A inputs with n finite output words each, then

\[
|\mathcal R_A(S)|\le\min(|S|,N^{nm}).
\]

Each endpoint supplies one signature, proving the first bound; each signature is a length-nm tuple from the N-word alphabet, proving the second. If the baseline is included, there are |R_A(S)|-1 distinct signatures different from it. Both bounds are about finite signatures, not a vector-space dimension or statistical information quantity. If some A executions fail, restrict to the total subset or explicitly change the output alphabet to include a failure symbol before applying a revised count.

**Canonical basis observation corollary.** Consider the direct full-output dense matrix graph with zero bias, separate nearest-even products/additions in fixed order, and canonical arithmetic zero. On a canonical standard basis input e_j, a finite stored endpoint returns precisely its j-th stored column. Indeed every product is either multiplication by zero or by one; after canonicalization the former is +0 and the latter is the original stored word. Every row accumulates only one possibly nonzero finite product and zeros, so its value is preserved exactly with no overflow. Therefore the raw signature on all e_1,...,e_d is injective on finite endpoint banks. For such A targets, response-image cardinality equals endpoint cardinality for every declared endpoint subset.

If instead every e_j belongs to the protected D raw domain under this same full-output zero-bias graph, equality of protected outputs forces equality of every stored column, hence E_BIT={w} when the no-op is admitted. This is a concrete full-format executable exhaustion condition. It requires those actual basis inputs and this graph; full numeric span alone is insufficient, as T6 shows. Nonzero bias, post-activation observations, restricted output ports or different arithmetic graphs require a separate argument. ∎

## T5 — Positive algebraic dimension can yield no nonzero stored ALG endpoint

Let M be the maximum finite positive value in either accepted format. Use an n-by-2 old matrix whose every row is (M,M), and let V=span(1,1). Then r=1 and U(V) has dimension n>0, yet among **all** admitted finite endpoint banks,

\[
\mathcal E_{\rm ALG}(W,V)=\{W\}.
\]

**Proof.** An exact protected row must satisfy w'_1+w'_2=2M. Every finite stored entry is at most M, so M-w'_1 and M-w'_2 are nonnegative. Their sum is zero, forcing both to be zero. The argument holds for every row. Thus only W is feasible, despite positive abstract dimension. ∎

This can be an actual finite protected-input fixture: use h=(1/2,1/2). Its span is V. Both old products equal M/2, exactly representable; their ordered sum equals M and remains finite. No overflowing old evaluation is needed for the impossibility. It is a theorem about stored **ALG** endpoints, not a claim that every possible BIT endpoint class is a singleton. It also differs from the A2 standalone-increment representability limitation: here the old bank's location at the finite endpoint boundary itself blocks every nonzero exact-safe replacement.

**Same rank, different old-bank capacity.** Keep V=span(1,1) and all canonical finite endpoints, but replace the old bank by the zero n-by-2 bank. The exact constraint on each replacement row is a+b=0. The canonical finite alphabet is closed under negation, with zero represented once. For each of its N values a, precisely the value -a is allowed in the second position. The rows are independent, giving exactly N^n stored ALG endpoints, compared with one at the saturated old bank. In the requested square 2-by-2 instance this is N^2 versus one with the same protected rank and the same abstract dimension two. This counts exact-safe stored endpoints; it does not reclassify every BIT endpoint. It demonstrates why old-bank location must be fixed in any finite-format capacity claim. ∎

## T6 — ALG and BIT capacity are not ordered by rank alone

**ALG without BIT.** In dimension three, take old zero weights and new Q, the BF16/FP32 rounded thirds projector with diagonal 2s and off-diagonal -s, where s=RN(1/3). Declare protected raw inputs 1_3 and eta 1_3, eta the format's smallest positive subnormal. Exact decoded Q annihilates their rank-one span. Under separate-RNE ordered execution it gives zero at 1_3 but eta in every coordinate at eta 1_3. Thus the new endpoint is in E_ALG but not E_BIT. The partial-sum/subnormal proof and actual A3 refusal carry over with the same graph and policy; no old-stage suite is rerun.

**BIT without ALG at full rank.** In dimension two, use old I_2 and new ((1,eta),(0,1)) on the complete protected set {0,(1,1),(1,0)}. Its span is K^2. At zero/e_1 the outputs agree exactly; at (1,1), RN(1+eta)=1 because eta is below half the spacing above one. Therefore every protected raw output agrees. The exact response at (1,1) nevertheless changes by (eta,0), so the edit is not in E_ALG. At legal A input e_2, output changes from (0,1) to (eta,1), with eta retained under gradual underflow. This is the supported A3 split-domain example, not a new universal hardware claim. ∎

Consequently neither E_ALG subset E_BIT nor E_BIT subset E_ALG holds generally. Full protected rank forces |E_ALG|=1 under the no-op premise, but does not force |E_BIT|=1. Neither rank r nor the dimension n(d-r) classifies finite observational capacity. The same numeric span can accompany different BIT constraints: adding e_2 to the raw protected set in the second example exposes the edit without increasing its already full rank.

**Even an individual BIT edit need not obey the algebraic rank bound.** On the duplicated-bit protected domain {0,(1,1)}, use old I_2 and new ((1,eta),(eta,1)). Each nonzero protected row evaluation rounds 1+eta back to one, while zero input returns canonical zeros. The stored effective difference ((0,eta),(eta,0)) has determinant -eta^2 nonzero, hence rank two. Yet d-r=1. At e_1 or e_2 an A output contains the retained eta change. The bound rank(Delta)<=d-r applies to exact annihilators, not to all finite-domain BIT edits. This is another conditional rounding example, not an assertion of robust or useful learning from the subnormal response. ∎

## T7 — Structural reservations and exact mask cost

Let J subset {1,...,d}, with t=|J|, and let M_J be all n-by-d maps supported only on columns J.

**Statement.** M_J is contained in U(V) iff every v in V has v_j=0 for all j in J. When this holds, dim M_J=nt, t<=c, and the loss relative to the unrestricted exact annihilator is n(c-t).

**Proof.** If all those coordinates vanish, every supported row has zero dot product with V. Conversely if v_j is nonzero for some j in J, the map having a single unit entry at row one, column j changes v, contradicting containment. The nt matrix-coordinate maps are independent and span M_J. Its inclusion in the nc-dimensional annihilator gives t<=c; subtracting dimensions gives the cost. ∎

For a general coordinate mask with allowed columns J_i in output row i, a row annihilates H precisely when its |J_i| free entries belong to ker(H[J_i,:]^T). Thus the masked exact dimension is

\[
\sum_{i=1}^{n}\bigl(|J_i|-\operatorname{rank}H[J_i,:]\bigr).
\]

The rows are independently constrained, so their nullities add. This formula permits correlated changes inside a row. Counting all writable entries as independent safe coordinates would be wrong unless the restricted protected matrices vanish. For target matrix X, the masked response dimension similarly equals the sum over rows of rank([H[J_i,:] X[J_i,:]])-rank(H[J_i,:]), by applying T3 to each row's restricted input space.

**A coordinate reservation can cost all geometric slack.** In K^2 with V=span(1,1), c=1 but neither coordinate is identically zero on V. No nonempty independently free reserved column set exists. Still the correlated row (a,-a) annihilates V. A geometric complement and an independently writable coordinate layout need not have equal freedom.

## T8 — A complete small layout budget and responding finite family

Use a shared rectangular bank

\[
W(\theta)=\begin{pmatrix}1&0&\theta_0\\0&1&\theta_1\end{pmatrix},
\]

with fixed first two columns and arbitrary admitted finite theta_0,theta_1. The fixed D arithmetic graph prepares either every (q,u,0), q,u Boolean, or the correlated set (q,q,0), and evaluates only the first two columns. A preceding pure endpoint validator may inspect all cells; on this admitted finite family it terminates and accepts without changing protected state. The A graph at legal e_3 reads each reserved coefficient, multiplies it by one and adds canonical zero. Bias is fixed zero. Numeric, graph, storage and scheduling policies remain fixed.

**Statement.** Every such endpoint is algebraically safe for the declared D domain and structurally BIT-safe under this graph. There are exactly N_F^2 endpoints and exactly N_F^2 distinct accepted finite A response signatures at e_3. From a baseline with theta=(0,0), there are N_F^2-1 changed signatures. The full Boolean D domain has rank two, geometric freedom 2(3-2)=2 and reservation freedom two; the correlated domain has rank one, geometric freedom 2(3-1)=4 but reservation freedom two, a cost of two abstract parameters.

**Proof.** Every protected input has third coordinate zero, so changes confined to that column annihilate it exactly. Stronger, the fixed sparse D arithmetic graph never reads the changed cells. After the admitted pure validation, identical protected inputs and fixed cells therefore yield identical protected arithmetic operands and results; the arithmetic read-exclusion proof is independent of the finite theta values. It does not assert identical validation branches, whole-host memory-access traces or elapsed time. Under any separately admitted surrounding state program satisfying A3's interface/invariant premises, the same local output/state argument can be lifted by its induction theorem.

Each theta coordinate has N_F independent canonical finite choices, so there are N_F^2 distinct stored endpoints. At A input e_3, multiplication by one preserves each finite value exactly, adding canonical zero preserves its canonical word, and no overflow occurs. Thus A output is exactly (theta_0,theta_1), giving an injective response map and the same cardinality. All-zero theta is one baseline signature; remove it for the changed count. The full domain includes e_1,e_2 and has rank two. The correlated domain spans (1,1,0) and has rank one. T1 and T7 give the stated dimensions and costs. ∎

This is a finite-format counting theorem plus an explicit small layout/graph construction, not an exhaustive execution of all N_F^2 pairs. Tests may enumerate each coordinate separately and selected pairs, but their actual scope is reported independently. It does not supply a general source-language compiler, prove an arbitrary optimized dense GEMM equivalent to the sparse graph, or certify native BF16/GPU hardware.

## Completion boundary of the mathematical results

The theorem families answer what algebraic rank and response budgets can support under stated constraints, and why storage/execution capacity needs separate definitions. They do not prove that a functional A change improves a learning objective. Counts, safe-set inclusions and rank constructions must retain their old-bank, domain, mask, graph and numerical assumptions. A4 does not carry out B4 long-term training, B1, C1 general compilation, C4 arbitrary hardware verification or full P2-C composition.
