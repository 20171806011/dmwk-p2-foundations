## Acceptance targets

For square d-by-d weights and protected rank r, prove exact update-space dimension d(d-r), individual rank at most d-r, and low-rank generator bound min(k,d-r), with explicit attainment constructions and conditions. Rectangular generalization must use a separate output dimension. Derive target-response capacity through the quotient of target inputs modulo protection; identify both nonzero-response existence and prescribed-response impossibility.

For finite formats, endpoints must be representable and updates are exact differences of endpoints. Define finite cardinality/response-image indicators and rigorous bounds or complete finite counts. Do not call a non-linear finite set a vector space. Increased protection is monotone only for genuinely nested domains/spaces with all other semantics fixed. Preserve A3's full-rank BIT-without-ALG counterexample and its implication for capacity definitions.

The safe mechanism must retain a nonzero stored patch with changed legal A output and protected D equality. Unknown/invalid evidence must refuse without publication. BF16 remains software RNE; CPU FP32 checks are local evidence; GPU/native BF16/general concurrency/arbitrary compiler optimization/full host-stack formal proof remain uncertified. No novelty, speed or universal learning claim is presumed.
