# P2-B4 mathematical checkpoint

Saved after reading the B4 plan and B3 handoff, before B4 implementation. Endpoints are decoded exactly; previous artifacts and suites remain untouched.

1. **Two indices and references.** W_k denotes the bank after k accepted updates, while t counts executed synchronous transitions. A declared schedule k(t) selects the bank snapshotted for transition t; updates preserve protected state and occur only at admitted serial boundaries. Comparisons distinguish W_k versus W_0 at the same activation, adjacent W_(k+1) versus W_k, and two scheduled execution histories. These are not interchangeable. Each actual increment is Delta_k=W_(k+1)-W_k after storage.

2. **Fixed protection and prefixes.** For a fixed globally justified V, the exact annihilator U(V) is linear. All Delta_k in U(V) iff every finite prefix W_K-W_0 belongs to U(V), by telescoping and adjacent differences. A safe final sum alone does not prove prefix safety: scalar W_0=0,W_1=1,W_2=0 cancels finally while violating protection at the middle endpoint. Reusing the same V consumes no new algebraic dimension merely because update count increases; finite storage, target response and graph BIT restrictions remain separate.

3. **Accumulating tasks and anchor compatibility.** With V_cum,K=sum_(i<=K)V_i, U(V_cum,K)=intersection_(i<=K)U(V_i), dimension n(d-r_K) and individual rank at most min(n,d-r_K). The exact target-response dimension for target matrix X is n(rank([H_K X])-r_K), with the inherited quotient relation condition. Compatible linear task anchors L_i require sum v_i=0 implies sum L_i(v_i)=0; then their common solutions form W_*+U(V_cum,K). Pairwise agreement alone can miss multi-task linear relations. A newly admitted task must be satisfied/checked at its admission endpoint; shrinking the update space cannot repair prior unsatisfied behavior or retroactively protect earlier history.

4. **Moving spaces and rank caps.** For nonnested V_k the available dimensions need not decrease, and updates safe only for the current V_k can damage historical tasks. Per-step rank<=q gives cumulative rank at most min(Kq,n,d-r) for fixed V, not q. Persistent low-rank residuals and successive update ranks are distinct. Storable endpoints and fixed-graph BIT classes are finite sets, not vector spaces of the same dimension; neither exact ALG nor BIT implies the other in general.

5. **Changing histories.** For declared old/reference and updated histories, certify each compared exact map on joint old/new domains with a common norm. If the updated exact map has cross-state gain L_t, same-input drift a_t relative to the stated reference, and two execution errors rho_t,rho'_t, then E_(t+1)<=L_t E_t+beta_t with beta_t=a_t+rho_t+rho'_t. The B3 product-sum applies only after this new per-step/domain proof. For affine same-input comparisons, W_(k(t))-W_0=sum_(i<k(t))Delta_i supplies an accumulated drift bound; direct net endpoint and disjoint increments are alternative bounds, not additive copies.

6. **Uniform switching and failure example.** A common invariant, uniform totality/input policy, bounded forcing and one common norm with L_t<=L<1 imply the uniform envelope max(E_0,beta/(1-L)). Stability of individual modes in separate norms does not suffice. A=((0,2),(0,0)) and B=A^T are individually nilpotent, but BA=diag(0,4), so alternating them amplifies suitable states by4 every two steps. This is a real exact switching counterexample; finite-format infinite execution still requires range/totality certification.

7. **Authorization and ledger.** A numerical switching-budget ledger is read-only and binds both endpoint histories, schedules, initial relation, domains, observations and per-prefix bounds. Final error cancellation cannot erase an earlier budget violation. Exact D transactions instead require B1/A3 complete-domain BIT (and the selected ALG condition), current version/full snapshot, all shared calls and numerical/schedule identity. Atomic serial publication includes authorized endpoint, optimizer/history/ledger state; rejection changes none of the protected owned snapshot. This proves only the stated trusted serial contract, not arbitrary concurrency, crash recovery or the C-series kernel.
