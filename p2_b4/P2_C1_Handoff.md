## Strict inherited conclusions

1. A fixed globally covering activation space V gives an annihilator of dimension n(d-r) when all output coordinates are protected. Repeated safe increments remain in it; each update does not spend another dimension. With a fixed absolute endpoint universe and fixed protected anchors, accepted ALG/BIT chains preserve their global feasible classes. Local optimizer/quantizer/step-cap stalls are separate from that global capacity.
2. Cumulative independent task constraints shrink the intersection, but task admission must also establish a compatible anchor at its actual admission endpoint. Merely freezing additional directions does not repair or retroactively protect an unsatisfied task. Nonnested moving spaces can change available directions and lose historical protection.
3. Per-step low rank is not a bound on cumulative residual rank. A persistent low-rank adapter, a fixed factor subspace and a sequence of low-rank increments are different constraints. Abstract response feasibility retains X alpha in V implies R alpha=0; target changes and stored realizability remain distinct.
4. Update index and execution time have separate schedules. Any changed endpoint version needs renewed graph/domain/constant evidence, or membership in a proved common family. B3's old coefficients are not automatically valid after a patch. Switched stability requires common-norm and common-domain premises; separate stable modes do not establish arbitrary-switching stability.
5. Actual stored differences already contain generation/projection/optimizer/quantization effects. Both compared executions' arithmetic errors remain in propagation. Direct net endpoint bounds and sums of disjoint increment contributions are alternative bounds, not additive copies.
6. Exact-D publication retains complete-domain BIT and the selected ALG condition, same-snapshot state semantics, fixed graph/numerical policy and all shared sites. Read-only numerical history receipts cannot authorize publication or weaken that contract. Rejection must preserve the entire owned bank, optimizer, state, counters and ledger under the specified serial model.

## Obligations for any C1 realization

Specify the source/graph/parameter identities and the permitted proof-producing transformations. A generic structural validator cannot infer that an external program's graph omits a call. Any new compiler/layout/domain therefore needs its own containment and graph-correspondence proof rather than a sample rank or inherited label.

State which observations are protected. Accepted patches can legitimately change weights, optimizer state, versions and learning records while preserving D's designated state/outputs; they do not make the entire metadata bank equal to an unpatched reference. Rejected requests must leave the entire owned snapshot unchanged. Trusted serial copy-and-publish behavior is not general concurrency, crash-consistency or hostile-memory protection.

Retain all actual failure evidence: fixed-space quantization stalls, incompatible tasks/targets, saturated anchors, moving-space historical damage, switched amplification, numerical-domain/budget failure, branch boundaries, subnormal/thirds and both-rounding terms. Preserve B1's 21 BIT-equal ALG-failing joint refusals and dense FP32 AdamW zero accepted updates in eight attempts with loss 5/2.

GPU, native BF16, arbitrary compiler/FMA/reassociation transformations, general concurrency, full host machine proof and universal learning performance remain unproved. C1 must state its own scope and tests; B4 supplies no blanket authorization or certification for those environments.
