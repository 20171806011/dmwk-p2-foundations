Cumulative new independent constraints can reduce capacity, while moving spaces can recover directions or lose historical guarantees. A final total increment annihilating V does not prove every earlier prefix was safe. Added tasks also need compatible target anchors and valid global activation/domain proofs; shrinking the update space alone does not retroactively satisfy a previously unprotected task.

Actual endpoints contain all upstream optimizer/projection/storage effects. Direct endpoint bounds and disjoint provenance ledgers are alternative accounting paths.
