## Strict starting premises

1. A globally valid protected activation set/span or a proved conservative superspace, merged across every protected use of each shared physical matrix. Samples cannot authorize a universal patch.
2. Fixed D, legal initial states and input histories, encoding, state invariants, deterministic logical schedule, and an explicit arithmetic graph/policy. If a patch can alter later activations, establish a whole-program invariant or a closed relational argument; original-program samples are insufficient.
3. A distinction among ideal update, generated candidate, stored replacement and exact decoded effective difference. Record both endpoints and reject nonfinite or unsupported operations according to the chosen contract.
4. An execution-level acceptance certificate, not only a real null product, numerical SVD cutoff, tiny residual, or zero execution on a generating basis.
5. At least one nonzero stored update with a changed admitted A response, plus precise protected-state and interface equality. Safety obtained only by rejecting every nonzero update is a different result.
